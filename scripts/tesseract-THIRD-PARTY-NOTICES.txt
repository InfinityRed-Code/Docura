Tesseract OCR as bundled with Docura PDF
========================================

This directory contains Tesseract OCR and the libraries it needs at runtime.
Docura PDF starts tesseract.exe as a separate program; it does not link any of
these files. All files are distributed unmodified.

Origin
------
Windows build of Tesseract 5.4.0 by UB Mannheim (built with MSYS2 / MINGW64):
  https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract
  release v5.4.0.20240606, tesseract-ocr-w64-setup-5.4.0.20240606.exe
Language models deu.traineddata and eng.traineddata from tessdata_fast,
tag 4.1.0:
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast

Only tesseract.exe, the DLLs it loads, tessdata\configs and the two language
models are included (see scripts/fetch-tesseract.ps1 in the Docura PDF
sources). Training tools and their libraries are left out.
